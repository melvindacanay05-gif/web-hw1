Short introduction

I love to cook. I take pride in trying to make everything from scratch so i can layer as many flavors as possible. If I can get to know you and what you like I could probably make the best dish you've ever had in your life. Unless you know any other cooks personally or Gordon Ramsay (pass on the LinkedIn) I can definitely throw down the meanest dish you've ever had.

Shrimp Tempura

My younger brother loves when I make this. A lot of American restaurants have no idea how to actually make authentic shrimp tempura, I wanted to know how so i learned it.

Macarons

Macarons taught me that there is no such thing as a hard skill. The hard part was actually setting my ego aside and being willing to learn from my mistakes.

Banh Mi

I made every part of this sandwich from scratch (kinda). Id say this is sort of my magnum opus as it combines offal (organs), pickling, bread making, charcuterie, and a lot of prep. Prep and mise en place are quite literally everything when it comes to cooking. Alot of the stuff people eat in upscale and fine dining are actually less fresh and made to order than all home cooked meals. Long story short, if you're cooking it at home it's fresh but if its made in a restaurant it isn't, most people wont bat an eye anyways.
